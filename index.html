    A clear read, not a silent repair.
    Choose the interpretation
    CSV dialects vary. Pick a delimiter, decide whether there is a header and compare the results. Quoted newlines and doubled quotes are supported.
    Keep the original intact
    RowBrief checks structure only. It does not correct data, infer types or execute formulas. Fatal quoting or size errors stop inspection; partial results are labeled.
    Share a smaller report
    The report omits content and names. Review it before sharing. No files, reports or preferences are stored by the app.
    Limits and practical details
    Strict UTF-8 only, with or without BOM. Up to 2 MiB, 10,000 parsed records and 100 fields per record; only the first 200 findings are listed. Blank records still count toward the record limit. Source lines and columns are one-based; columns count Unicode code points. CRLF is one line break. A leading BOM is excluded from visible column positions.
    Headers are checked for empty values and exact duplicates. Field counts are compared with the first included record even when the header option is off. No business-rule, data-type, encoding-conversion, formula-safety or universal spreadsheet-compatibility check is provided. A first page load requires a connection unless you serve your own local copy. No offline cache is installed.
